## 01 — Roof tile (reversal chain)
- **Sep 8, HOA:** clay tile required.
- **Sep 10, Builder:** clay tile is in current scope and price.
- **Oct 2, Builder:** clay tile needs structural upgrade, +$38,000 and ~3 weeks. *Contradicts Sep 10.*
- **Oct 14, HOA:** clay tile no longer required; Class A composite allowed. *Reverses Sep 8.*
- **Open questions:** Does the $38k change order still apply now that tile isn't required? Get the HOA's reversal in writing as a formal approval.

## 02 — Like-for-like eligibility (builder vs city)
- **Aug 19, Builder:** room over garage still qualifies as like-for-like, no extra review.
- **Oct 21, City:** height goes from 18'6" to 24'0", over 110%, so it does NOT qualify under EO1.
- **Why it matters:** the 110% rule covers height as well as footprint. The builder only looked at footprint.
- **Open questions:** Redesign to stay within 110%, or go the EO8 route? What's the schedule and cost impact, and who pays for the redesign?

- **Open questions:** Ask the insurer to reconcile its Jun 3 statement in writing. Get the city's correction notice to show the items are code-required.

## 04 — Fence (HOA vs city fire rule)
- **Oct 5, HOA:** redwood fence connected to the house.
- **Oct 16, City/LAFD:** nothing combustible within 5 ft of the house; the connection must be metal or masonry.
- **Oct 17, Builder:** HOA approval takes priority; will build in redwood to the house. *Conflicts with the fire rule.*
- **Open questions:** Ask the HOA whether a metal or masonry section for the first 5 ft is acceptable. Tell the builder not to proceed until resolved.
- **Note:** Homeward should flag the conflict, not decide who's right (non-goal #1).
